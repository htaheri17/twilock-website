INTERNAL DESKTOP TOOL

Twilock Slideshow Inbox use terms

These terms describe the intended use of the internal desktop tool operated by Hussain Taheri. It is not currently offered as a public signup service, and these terms do not replace the separate license for the Twilock iPhone app.

Authorized use

The tool is for the operator’s authorized TikTok accounts and content they own or have permission to use. The operator must review the selected account, exact slide order, title, caption, hashtags and required disclosures before requesting a draft upload.

Draft uploads and publishing

The tool uses TikTok’s official photo upload mode to send a draft for further editing in the TikTok app. It does not directly publish or schedule a post. The account owner chooses any music and final settings and decides whether to publish. TikTok’s own terms, content rules, account eligibility and API limits also apply.

Availability and approval

TikTok production access has not been approved as of October 24, 2026. A Sandbox demonstration does not establish production access. Upload processing and inbox delivery depend on TikTok and the image host; the tool does not guarantee approval, delivery times, views or business outcomes. It avoids automatic resubmission when a response is uncertain.

Data practices

The desktop tool privacy notice explains local storage, TikTok authorization and the public photo-hosting step. Only content intended for that hosting step should be deployed.
